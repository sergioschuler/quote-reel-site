Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy covers the Quote Reel for Kindle browser extension, Android app and this website. Publisher: Sergio Schuler. Contact: s@productleadership.io.

What the apps handle

When you request an export, the extension reads book titles, authors, Amazon book identifiers, book and cover URLs, highlights, related notes, available page/location information and export metadata from your signed-in Amazon notebook.

Google authorization gives the apps access to your selected Drive account identifier and email address, and limited access to files this integration creates or opens. The apps use Google’s drive.file permission, not access to all of your Drive files. Google manages sign-in; you do not enter your Amazon or Google password into Quote Reel.

Why and where data is used

The extension uploads a complete JSON snapshot to your selected Google Drive account, in Kindle Quotes/latest.json. A successful export replaces that file. This legacy folder name remains so existing installs keep working.

The Android app reads that export to display highlights and notes and keeps the last validated snapshot in private app storage for offline reading. The saved snapshot is bound to the selected Drive account and excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Account identity and the last successful sync time are stored locally.

The extension stores selected account identity, status and export summaries locally, and keeps one pending export in local IndexedDB for retry. Its access token is held in browser session storage; Android handles access tokens in memory. The apps do not log tokens or send them to Amazon cover hosts.

Services that receive data

Amazon receives notebook requests made through your existing signed-in browser session. Google receives authorization and Drive API requests, account-related information and the snapshot you choose to upload. Android requests book covers from Amazon image hosts; those hosts receive ordinary network request information, such as your IP address and the requested image URL.

These services process information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Amazon Privacy Notice. There is no Quote Reel developer server receiving your highlights or notes. We do not sell your reading data or use it for advertising. The browser extension has no analytics. Android version 0.5.0 and later offers optional usage analytics, described below; earlier versions have no analytics integration.

Quote Reel’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used to provide the export and reading functionality you request.

Optional Android usage analytics

In Android version 0.5.0 and later, you can choose whether to allow usage analytics through Amplitude. Collection is off until you allow it. Analytics helps us understand onboarding and reading features. Events describe app starts/backgrounding, onboarding page views and button actions, successful connections/imports, reader sessions, highlight views/pauses, manual navigation, book taps and menu actions.

Events include a random installation identifier, timestamps and session identifiers, app/build and SDK versions, platform, OS/device information and language. Depending on the event, they include first-recorded-use flags, feed mode, button/menu choices, navigation direction, import source, text word count, whether a note exists, and foreground duration. They do not include highlight or note text, book titles/authors/identifiers, Google email/account identity, Drive file identifiers, or authorization tokens. We disable advertising/app-set identifiers, location and IP-based analytics enrichment, automatic screen/interaction capture, diagnostic telemetry and session replay. Amplitude still receives normal network request information when events are sent.

Amplitude processes these analytics in its US region. See the Amplitude Privacy Notice. You can turn off future collection in Usage analytics, available from the reader menu or onboarding information dialog. The app may upload events previously collected with your permission; changing the setting or disconnecting Google Drive does not erase previously collected analytics. Installation analytics settings remain separate from Drive/account settings.

Analytics is retained by Amplitude under the applicable plan and service retention controls until removed. To request deletion of your analytics, email s@productleadership.io with the Analytics ID shown in Usage analytics. We use this ID to locate the installation data and submit a deletion request through Amplitude. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes the local identifier, so save it before doing so if you want to request deletion. The Android app works without allowing analytics.

Retention and deletion

  • A pending browser export remains until a successful upload, replacement by a later export, or disconnect. Disconnecting the extension removes its pending export and connected account settings and stops future uploads.
  • The Android snapshot remains until replaced by a successful sync, disconnected, or removed with app data/uninstallation. A failed sync keeps the last valid copy. Disconnecting Android deletes its saved snapshot and account preferences.
  • Drive files remain in your account until you delete them. Disconnecting or uninstalling does not delete those files. Google may retain previous versions or deleted items according to its retention controls.
  • Disconnect does not revoke Google’s authorization. Revoke it in your Google Account’s third-party access settings if desired. The extension and Android app use the same Google Cloud project, so revocation can affect both.

This website and contacting support

This static website has no sign-in, forms, tracking scripts or analytics. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your email address and message are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords, authorization tokens or full personal exports. If a small excerpt is needed to investigate a bug, remove private text first. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address above, subject to any applicable retention obligations.
