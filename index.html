From your notebook to your phone.

  1. Sign in to Amazon on your phone.

    Sync your Kindle over Wi-Fi, then sign in on Amazon’s own page inside Quote Reel.

  2. Import your highlights.

    Your Amazon.com notebook is saved privately on your phone for offline reading. No desktop computer, browser extension or Google Drive required.

  3. Read at your own pace.

    Swipe between highlights, tap to pause, or tap a book to read its highlights in order. Notes appear alongside the highlight when present.

A few things to know.

The Android app requires Android 9 or later. Refresh from Amazon while reading; opening the app also refreshes when the last successful import is over 24 hours old. Keep the app open during import. Failed refreshes leave saved highlights unchanged. The browser extension is retired. If you used an earlier Drive-based version, update the Android app and sign in to Amazon again. Your existing Drive exports remain yours; the current app does not use them.

Only highlights and notes available in Amazon’s online notebook can be imported. Opening a book in the Kindle app does not jump directly to the highlighted passage. There is no advertising. Usage analytics, crash diagnostics and masked session replays in supported builds are enabled by default. Turn collection off in Settings → Usage & crash reports. Your highlight and note text is never sent to analytics; book details and private reading content are protected in replays. See our privacy policy.
