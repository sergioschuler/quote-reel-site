Your Kindle highlights and personal notes, brought back into view in a quiet Android reading feed.

Currently in private testing.

From your notebook to your phone.

  1. Export on your computer.

    Sync your Kindle with Amazon. Open your Amazon notebook and use the Quote Reel browser extension to fetch and upload your highlights to your own Google Drive.

  2. Connect on Android.

    Use the same Google account in the Android app. Your last valid export stays available for offline reading.

  3. Read at your own pace.

    Swipe between highlights, tap to pause, or tap a book to read its highlights in order. Notes appear alongside the highlight when present.

A few things to know.

The extension needs a signed-in Amazon notebook and Google Drive authorization. The Android app requires Android 9 or later with Google Play services. New highlights arrive after you run another export; this is not continuous background Kindle synchronization.

Only annotations available in Amazon’s online notebook can be exported. Opening a book in the Kindle app does not jump directly to the highlighted passage. There is no advertising. Android version 0.5.0 and later offers optional usage analytics; your highlight and note text is never sent to the analytics service. See our privacy policy.
