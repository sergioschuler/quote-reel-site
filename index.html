From your notebook to your phone.

  1. Sign in to Amazon on your phone.

    Sync your Kindle over Wi-Fi, then sign in on Amazon’s own page inside Quote Reel.

  2. Import your highlights.

    Your Amazon.com notebook is saved privately on your phone for offline reading. Android 0.9.0 and later needs no desktop extension or Google Drive.

  3. Read at your own pace.

    Swipe between highlights, tap to pause, or tap a book to read its highlights in order. Notes appear alongside the highlight when present.

A few things to know.

The Android app requires Android 9 or later. Refresh from Amazon while reading; opening the app also refreshes when the last successful import is over 24 hours old. Keep the app open during import. Failed refreshes leave saved highlights unchanged. The separate extension and earlier Android versions use Drive exports.

Only annotations available in Amazon’s online notebook can be exported. Opening a book in the Kindle app does not jump directly to the highlighted passage. There is no advertising. Android version 0.5.0 and later includes usage analytics and crash diagnostics, enabled by default with a setting to turn both off; your highlight and note text is never sent to the analytics service.
